TITLE (≤60):
What If Your Bank Sent You $10 Million By Mistake? #shorts

DESCRIPTION:
In 2021 a crypto company meant to refund a woman in Melbourne 100 Australian dollars. A worker typed her account number into the amount box and she received about 10.5 million instead. Nobody noticed for seven months. She bought a mansion with a gym and a cinema, then an audit found the mistake and a court made her pay it back with interest.
