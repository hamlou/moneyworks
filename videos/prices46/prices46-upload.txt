TITLE (≤60):
What If Prices Doubled Every Single Day? #shorts

DESCRIPTION:
In Hungary in 1946 prices did worse than double: they tripled every day. The government printed a banknote with twenty zeros, then threw the whole currency away. One new forint was worth 4 followed by 29 zeros of old pengő, and street cleaners swept the old banknotes off the road.

Sources:
S1 Wikipedia, Hungarian pengő + Guinness World Records 'Highest inflation rate': July 1946 daily inflation about 207 % (prices x3 a day, doubling about every 15 hours) — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_peng%C5%91
S2 same: largest note 100 quintillion (10^20) pengő; 1 forint = 4 x 10^29 pengő (Aug 1946)
S3 Daily News Hungary, 'Hungary's nightmare hyperinflation of 1946' (wages spent at once; street cleaners swept worthless notes) — https://dailynewshungary.com/hungarys-nightmare-hyperinflation-of-1946/
