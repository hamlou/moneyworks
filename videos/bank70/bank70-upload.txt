TITLE (≤60):
What If Every Bank Closed For Six Months? #shorts

DESCRIPTION:
In 1970 every bank in Ireland closed for about six months because of a strike. People did not panic: around 11,000 pubs acted like banks, people wrote cheques on any scrap of paper and passed them around like money. When the banks reopened there was a backlog of about £5 billion in cheques.

Sources:
S1 Wikipedia, 'Irish bank strikes (1966–1976)' + Bank Underground, 'The Cheque Republic' (2016) — https://bankunderground.co.uk/2016/01/20/the-cheque-republic-money-in-a-modern-economy-with-no-banks/
S2 Business Insider (2016) citing the Central Bank of Ireland: 11,000 pubs and 12,000 shops acted as bank substitutes — https://www.businessinsider.com/pubs-replaced-banks-in-ireland-in-1970-and-the-economy-was-fine-2016-1
