# Why Is Your Rent So Expensive?

## HOOK
- `k1` Rent rose faster than paychecks in eighty eight percent of counties.
- `k2` And twenty two million renters paid the price.
- `k3` So why is your rent so expensive?
- `k4` You will see who profits and the one secret that fixes it.

## THE NOTICE
- `c1_01` Every month I open my mailbox and my landlord leaves me a little surprise envelope.
- `c1_02` My rent goes up another fifty dollars while my salary stays completely flat.
- `c1_03` That rent notice in my hand is not just my personal problem.
- `c1_04` Between two thousand and twenty twenty rent grew faster than income for almost every family.
- `c1_05` Eighty eight percent of counties saw rents sprint ahead while wages crawled.
- `c1_06` So tenants ended up spending over thirty percent of their whole income just on shelter.
- `c1_07` That makes you house poor before you even buy groceries.
- `c1_08` And it gets worse when you see what happened to the actual houses.

## THE GAP
- `c2_01` Imagine ten people standing in line to rent five available apartments.
- `c2_02` The landlord does not even have to try because five people will fight for every door.
- `c2_03` Between two thousand and twenty twenty housing demand in America jumped by twenty six percent.
- `c2_04` But the actual supply of homes only grew by nineteen percent.
- `c2_05` Have a guess how big that shortage really is.
- `c2_06` That seven percent gap means millions of families are fighting over the same keys.
- `c2_07` When supply falls behind demand the landlord can name any price and someone has to pay it.
- `c2_08` That is the math that keeps your rent notice climbing every single year.
- `c2_09` And while tenants fight over old apartments big investment firms entered the game.
- `c2_10` My landlord even sent me two rent notices this month just to be sure.

## THE INVESTORS
- `c3_01` Wall Street funds realized that buying neighborhood homes was safer than the stock market.
- `c3_02` They bought thousands of single family houses all across the country.
- `c3_03` Corporate owners care about rental yields so they raise rents aggressive and fast.
- `c3_04` When one big fund owns thirty homes in your neighborhood they control the local market price.
- `c3_05` You are not negotiating with a sweet neighbor named Bob anymore.
- `c3_06` You are negotiating with an algorithm designed to maximize profit.
- `c3_07` My rent notice is now signed by a corporation four states away.
- `c3_08` Which brings us back to that landlord envelope from the beginning.
- `c3_09` Because corporate funds love shortages and there is only one thing that breaks their power.
- `c3_10` And it is a simple rule that cities keep fighting over.

## THE FIX
- `c4_01` So why is your rent so expensive?
- `c4_02` You would think landlords just got greedy overnight.
- `c4_03` Nope because greed only works when people have no other place to go.
- `c4_04` The real systemic cause is a massive shortage of built homes.
- `c4_05` When cities build new apartments a one percent increase in supply lowers average rent by zero point nineteen percent.
- `c4_06` More apartments on the market means landlords have to compete for you instead of you competing for them.
- `c4_07` I finally put my monthly rent notice where it belongs and called city hall.
- `c4_08` Tell me in the comments how much your rent went up this year.
