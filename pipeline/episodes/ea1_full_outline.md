# ea1 FULL VIDEO (10 min) — outline to build ONLY after the owner approves the 1-minute test
"What would actually change if Abdul El-Sayed became president?" — Dave plays it out as a what-if, chapter by chapter
("Day 1 / Week 1 / Year 1…"), using ONLY his stated plans and documented reactions. Neutral: we show what he proposes,
who opposes it and why (their own words), and what the system (Congress, courts) would allow. Never our verdict.

Source quality rule for this topic: a fact needs a mainstream / non-partisan source (AP, NBC, NPR, Bridge Michigan, Michigan
Public, Britannica, Wikipedia-with-citation, the campaign's own site for "he proposes X"). Items marked ⚠ come from partisan or
single sources → verify with a second source before scripting, or drop.
