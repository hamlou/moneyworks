## Chapter 1 — DAY ONE: YOUR HEALTH BILL (≈ 1:00–3:00)
⟨calendar slams: DAY 1 · scoreboard card: PROMISES 0 · LAWS PASSED 0⟩

So it is day one and President El-Sayed walks into the office and the first thing on the desk is the plan he talks about the most which is Medicare for All. [S5]

The idea is simple to say and very hard to do because the government becomes everybody's basic health insurance and the doctor bill goes to Washington instead of to you. [S5]

His campaign says you could still keep extra insurance from your job or your union but it should become redundant and unnecessary. [S5]

⟨price tag counter rolls⟩ Now the price. When Bernie Sanders had a plan like this in 2020 the Urban Institute said it would cost the federal government thirty four trillion dollars over ten years. [S8]

And a fellow at that same institute told reporters this year that with inflation it could now cost close to double that. [S8]

**Sixty something trillion. I checked my wallet. Not enough.** ⟨Dave opens wallet, one moth flies out⟩

So who pays? Here it gets honest because El-Sayed has said he would try to make the rich pay and that he hopes to avoid raising taxes on most families including people who earn around a hundred thousand dollars a year. [S9]

But his campaign did not say exactly how it would pay for it and a budget watchdog group called the Committee for a Responsible Federal Budget said back in 2019 that taxes on the middle class would have to rise to pay for Medicare for All. [S9]

That same group also said that a lot of families could end up paying less in taxes than they pay today in premiums and copays. [S9]

⟨two-sided scale: TAXES ↑ vs PREMIUMS ↓, it wobbles⟩ So your paycheck might lose a piece on one side and win a bigger piece on the other side and nobody can tell you which one until the plan has real numbers. [S9]

Bridge Michigan checked it and their bottom line was that the claim it would raise taxes is likely correct but some experts think the total cost could go down for some families. [S9]

**So day one ends with one plan and zero laws. Hold that thought.**
