# Episode 56 — vertical short storyboard

## Story
Dave sells a $30 glowing cat lamp via dropshipping, but a $12 supplier cost, payment fees, $25 customer-acquisition cost, refunds and chargebacks leave him down $1,546 despite $3,000 in sales.

## Visual rules
- Frame zero starts with Dave, a real product/e-commerce photo, and an original meme card all visible.
- Every real-photo shot must also show Dave or Bob and a third object: a meme/reaction card. No photo-only shots.
- Every image is shown once and must directly match its narration beat.
- Captions are phrase-by-phrase, each phrase on its own line; never a paragraph block.
- Subscribe CTA appears twice, placed in safe areas away from faces.
- Story is a compressed short, not the full long episode.

## Beat map
- Hook: $3,000 sales versus -$1,546 bank balance.
- Product + supplier: glowing cat lamp and shipping box, Dave reacts with “WAITER WHO NEVER SAW THE KITCHEN”.
- Ads: phone/ad dashboard and “PAYING FOR EYEBALLS”.
- Conversion math: calculator and “50 CLICKS = $25”.
- Who wins: warehouse/platform and “THEY GET PAID EITHER WAY”.
- Fix: calculator + product comparison and “KNOW YOUR AD CEILING”.
- Ending: Dave shuts laptop, “SALES ≠ PROFIT”, CTA.
