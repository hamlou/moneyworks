# Episode 56 — vertical short storyboard

## Story
Dave sells a $30 glowing cat lamp through dropshipping. The $12 supplier cost, payment fees, $25 customer-acquisition cost, refunds and chargebacks leave him down $1,546 despite $3,000 in sales.

## Visual rules
- Dave is the only character in this short. Do not add Bob or a second character just to fill space.
- Match the long-form Episode 56: Dave reacts like a person to the exact thing happening. Use one clear action, then a meaningful reaction only when the story changes. No idle walking, random waving, or constant pose cycling.
- Frame zero immediately presents the contradiction: $3,000 in sales, then the bank balance drops to -$1,546. Dave's expression changes from proud to stunned.
- Every real-photo shot shows Dave plus one separate, readable meme/reaction card. The photo must directly match the spoken line.
- Use the cat lamp, store/order, parcel, payment fee, ad, ad dashboard, warehouse, returns, calculator, product search, and laptop photos only at their matching beats. Do not reuse an image.
- Memes should be short, specific, and readable on a phone—not generic labels or paragraphs. Keep faces clear.
- Captions stay in the lower safe area and never cover Dave's face or the key number.
- Subscribe CTA appears twice, away from Dave and the meme.

## Beat map
- Hook: $3,000 sales versus -$1,546 bank balance; instant visual contradiction.
- Product + supplier: the $30 glowing cat lamp and $12 supplier cost.
- Payment fee: show exactly what remains before ads.
- Ads: show the click cost and the 100-click / 2-buyer conversion.
- Returns: late parcels trigger refund and chargeback risk.
- Fix: compare the $16.80 break-even ad ceiling with the actual $25 acquisition cost.
- Ending: Dave shuts the laptop; sales are not profit.
