# SHORT — Episode 57: “Your Brother Pays Everyone Except You”
Source: `pipeline/episodes/ep57-short.json`
Format: standalone vertical Short, 1080×1920, real Andrew voiceover, Dave/Danny stickman cast.
Target runtime: ~85–95 seconds.

## Hook — first 0.1 seconds
**VO:** “Your brother owes you three thousand dollars—but somehow, every bill gets paid except yours.”
**Frame 0:** Real family meal photo slams in already moving. Giant `$3,000` and `YOU GET $0 BACK` stamps land immediately with a hit SFX. No logo, no fade, no intro.
**Next cut:** A unique payment photo; Dave appears in the same frame, facepalming beside “EXCEPT YOURS.”
**Escalation:** Car-payment photo → credit-card payment photo → the tire blowout. Every first-six-second cut must add information, not merely zoom.

## Story / visual map
- **Hook:** Family owes $3,000; every other bill is paid. Promise the viewer the repayment fix.
- **The promise:** Real repayment-plan photo, Dave and Danny appear together, “ONE QUESTION.”
- **The trust mistake:** Send-app animation; pizza-emoji contract gag. Dave reacts.
- **Why Dave gets postponed:** Unique creditor/phone photo with Danny, then paid-bill stack and Dave’s speech bubble.
- **The personal cost:** Unique repair/tire photo with Dave; $400 charge and damage meter.
- **Audience interaction:** Guess card, then a unique cash/survey photo with Dave reacting; cite Bankrate 2025 in on-screen text.
- **Meme beat 1:** Dave facepalms while Danny shrugs at “ME: PAY ME FRIDAY / BRO: NEXT FRIDAY BRO.” This is an original channel meme, not a random unrelated image.
- **Written agreement:** Unique loan-note photo; Dave points at the “LOAN ≠ GIFT” reveal.
- **Fix:** Unique signed-agreement photo, followed by a unique autopay/banking photo with both characters; show $125/month, payday timing and 24 months.
- **Rule:** “GIFT OR LOAN?” and only lend what you can afford to lose. Unique family photo with Dave.
- **Ending:** Dave waves beside a unique bank-transfer photo; “ONE QUESTION. ONE PAGE.” and a concise subscribe CTA.

## Mixed-media guardrails
- Fourteen unique photo IDs in `pipeline/photos/ep57.json`; each ID is used once in the timeline. Never reuse a photo, even with a different crop/label.
- Keep a drawn character in the same frame as at least 80% of real-photo scenes. Frame 0 is the only planned character-free photo shot, per PLAYBOOK §0.10.
- Photos must directly match the spoken beat; no generic stock-image filler.
- Aim for a meaningful change every 1–3 seconds, and 4+ changes in the first 6 seconds.
- CTA reminder after the mid-story reveal and a short CTA at the end.
