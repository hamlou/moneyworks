Format: vertical short (Reels/TikTok/YouTube Shorts), 9:16, same Dave/Bob stickman cast and voice as the long-form channel.
Target runtime: ~90s. Target words: ~204 (shorts run faster than long-form; v1 draft at 247 words rendered to 115s, so this cut trims ~20%).

## SCRIPT

**[~0:04] — Rewind**
**VO:** "Six months earlier, he signed one paper for his buddy Bob."
**Visual:** "6 MONTHS EARLIER" stamp. Dave and Bob at the car lot; Bob gives a thumbs up, a paper reading "SIGN HERE X".

**[~0:08] — The bait**
**VO:** "Bob needed a car. Bad credit. So Dave co-signed an eighteen thousand dollar loan."
**Visual:** "BAD CREDIT" stamp on Bob, then a card: **"THE LOAN — $18,000."**

**[~0:13] — The reveal nobody explains**
**VO:** "A co-signer isn't a reference. It's a second borrower. Same eighteen thousand dollars."
**Visual:** Card: "NOT a reference" → "A SECOND BORROWER" → "$18,000" in red.

**[~0:18] — The ghost debt**
**VO:** "Three months later, Dave's own mortgage application flags it: a four hundred dollar car payment."
**Visual:** Credit-report monitor flashes "$400/mo" in red. Stamp: "WHOSE IS THIS?"

**[~0:23] — The gut-punch**
**VO:** "Bob's car. Dave's debt too."
**Visual:** Dave facepalms. Card: "BOB'S CAR." / "DAVE'S DEBT." (red).

**[~0:27] — Bob goes quiet**
**VO:** "Then Bob loses that job, and stops paying."
**Visual:** Bob, sad, speech bubble: "can't pay right now..."

**[~0:31] — The call comes straight to Dave**
**VO:** "The debt collector calls Dave directly. The lender doesn't even have to chase Bob first."
**Visual:** Loop back to hook shot — red "DEBT COLLECTOR · now — calling YOU directly" banner, camera shake.

**[~0:37] — Why the lender smiled**
**VO:** "One signature. Two wallets. One car."
**Visual:** Split card: one car, two labeled wallets — "BOB" and "DAVE."

**[~0:41] — First blood**
**VO:** "Dave pays twelve hundred dollars, just to make it stop."
**Visual:** Card: "DAVE PAYS — $1,200" (red).

**[~0:45] — The low point (letter + scary math, merged)**
**VO:** "Then the repossession letter: owe fifteen thousand, sell for eight, Dave could owe seven thousand more."
**Visual:** "REPOSSESSION" stamp, then napkin math cascades: "owe $15,000" → "- sell $8,000" → "gap: $7,000" (red). HUD ("Dave's Debt") bumps to a worst-case "-$8,200".

**[~0:52] — The turn**
**VO:** "So Dave and Bob make a plan. Bob sells it himself, and covers what's left."
**Visual:** Dave and Bob talking it through; bubble: "sell it myself, cover the rest."

**[~0:57] — The payoff**
**VO:** "Loan closed. Damage stops at twelve hundred. The seven thousand dollar gap never happens."
**Visual:** Green card: "LOAN CLOSED — $1,200." Gold stamp: "$7,000 AVOIDED." HUD relief: back down to "-$1,200".

**[~1:02] — Digging out the paper**
**VO:** "That night, Dave finds the paper he signed. One sentence was already on it."
**Visual:** Dave reading an old lined paper.

**[~1:07] — The reveal that predicted everything**
**VO:** "If the borrower doesn't pay, you will have to. It had predicted everything." *(verbatim FTC Notice to Cosigner language, 16 CFR 444.3)*
**Visual:** The sentence types/reveals onto the paper in red; Dave shocked.

**[~1:12] — The rule**
**VO:** "So here's the rule: before you co-sign anything, ask, could I pay this whole loan, alone?"
**Visual:** Card: "THE RULE: 'Could I pay this whole loan, alone?'"

**[~1:17] — The soft landing**
**VO:** "If not, find another way to help your friend."
**Visual:** Dave and Bob, both waving, on good terms.

**[~1:20] — CTA (self-contained, no "part 2")**
**VO:** "Subscribe. It's the only paper we'll ever ask you to sign."
**Visual:** Navy card: "SUBSCRIBE — the only paper we'll ask you to sign."
