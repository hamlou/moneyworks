## SCRIPT

**[0:04–0:08] — Rewind**
**VO:** "Six months earlier, he signed one piece of paper. For his buddy Bob."
**Visual:** "6 MONTHS EARLIER" stamp. Dave and Bob at the car lot; Bob gives a thumbs up, a paper reading "SIGN HERE X".

**[0:08–0:14] — The bait**
**VO:** "Bob needed a car for a new job. Bad credit. So Dave co-signed an eighteen thousand dollar loan."
**Visual:** "BAD CREDIT" stamp on Bob, then a card: **"THE LOAN — $18,000."**

**[0:14–0:20] — The reveal nobody explains**
**VO:** "A co-signer isn't a reference. It's a second borrower. Same debt. All eighteen thousand dollars."
**Visual:** Card: "NOT a reference" → "A SECOND BORROWER" → "$18,000" in red.

**[0:20–0:27] — The ghost debt**
**VO:** "Three months later, Dave applies for his own mortgage. The loan officer frowns: whose four hundred dollar payment is this?"
**Visual:** Credit-report monitor flashes "$400/mo" in red. Stamp: "WHOSE IS THIS?"

**[0:27–0:32] — The gut-punch**
**VO:** "It's Bob's car. But now it's Dave's debt too."
**Visual:** Dave facepalms. Card: "BOB'S CAR." / "DAVE'S DEBT." (red).

**[0:32–0:36] — Bob goes quiet**
**VO:** "Then Bob loses that job. And stops paying."
**Visual:** Bob, sad, speech bubble: "can't pay right now..."

**[0:36–0:42] — The call comes straight to Dave**
**VO:** "The debt collector calls Dave directly. The lender doesn't even have to chase Bob first."
**Visual:** Loop back to hook shot — red "DEBT COLLECTOR · now — calling YOU directly" banner, camera shake.

**[0:42–0:47] — Why the lender smiled**
**VO:** "One signature gave the lender two wallets. For one car."
**Visual:** Split card: one car, two labeled wallets — "BOB" and "DAVE."

**[0:47–0:52] — First blood**
**VO:** "Dave pays twelve hundred dollars, just to stop it. For a car he's never driven."
**Visual:** Card: "DAVE PAYS — $1,200" (red).

**[0:52–0:58] — The letter**
**VO:** "Then the letter arrives. Repossession. If the sale doesn't cover the loan, Dave covers the gap."
**Visual:** Red "REPO NOTICE" paper card.

**[0:58–1:05] — The scary math (the low point)**
**VO:** "Owe fifteen thousand. Sells for eight. Dave could owe seven thousand more. For a car that's gone."
**Visual:** Napkin math: "owe $15,000" → "- sells $8,000" → "gap: $7,000" (red).

**[1:05–1:10] — The turn**
**VO:** "So Dave and Bob make a plan. Bob sells it himself, before the deadline, and covers what's left."
**Visual:** Dave and Bob talking it through; bubble: "sell it myself, cover the rest."

**[1:10–1:16] — The payoff**
**VO:** "The loan closes. Damage stops at twelve hundred. The seven thousand dollar gap never happens."
**Visual:** Green card: "LOAN CLOSED — $1,200." Gold stamp: "$7,000 AVOIDED."

**[1:16–1:21] — Digging out the paper**
**VO:** "That night, Dave finds the paper he signed. One sentence was already on it."
**Visual:** Dave reading an old lined paper.

**[1:21–1:27] — The reveal that predicted everything**
**VO:** "If the borrower doesn't pay, you will have to. It had predicted everything." *(verbatim FTC Notice to Cosigner language, 16 CFR 444.3)*
**Visual:** The sentence types/reveals onto the paper in red; Dave shocked.

**[1:27–1:33] — The rule**
**VO:** "So here's the rule. Before you co-sign anything, ask: could I pay this whole loan, alone?"
**Visual:** Card: "THE RULE: 'Could I pay this whole loan, alone?'"

**[1:33–1:37] — The soft landing**
**VO:** "If the answer's no, find another way to help your friend."
**Visual:** Dave and Bob, both waving, on good terms.

**[1:37–1:42] — CTA (self-contained, no "part 2")**
**VO:** "Subscribe. It's the only paper we'll ever ask you to sign."
**Visual:** Navy card: "SUBSCRIBE — the only paper we'll ask you to sign."
