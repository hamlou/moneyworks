**VO:** "Meanwhile Bob — down three thousand dollars — gets a text from his VIP host. Bonus bets. Free tickets. One big study found half a percent of customers make seventy percent of a sportsbook's money. The app doesn't want winners. It wants losers it can keep feeding."
