## 5 proposals (owner approves before any script)

**1. Why Saving Money Is Making You Poorer**
- Angle borrowed from: *LITTLE BIT BETTER* ("Once You Understand MONEY, You'll Stop Saving It") and *Ray Dalio* (structural illusion of fiat currency).
- Hook (first 10 s, Dave speaking, natural sentences): Every dollar you lock inside a bank account silently loses a tiny piece of its purchasing power while you sleep. The financial system was built to reward people who move money, which means quietly sitting on cash guarantees you lose.
- Why a viewer thinks "that's MY problem": I’ve been trying to save cash in my bank account for years, but things just keep getting harder to afford.
- Payoff withheld until the end: The hidden rule of inflation—how banks use your stagnant savings to build their own wealth while your money melts, and the exact shift to asset ownership that stops the leak.

**2. Why You Pay Twice for Everything You Buy**
- Angle borrowed from: *Honest Ads* ("Why Credit Cards Are A Scam") and *Sketchinance* ("The Salary Trap").
- Hook (first 10 s, Dave speaking, natural sentences): When you swipe a card for something you can't afford today, you aren't just spending cash—you're trading away hours of your future life. That simple transaction locks you into working next month's job just to pay off last month's choices.
- Why a viewer thinks "that's MY problem": I feel trapped working a job I dislike just to keep up with monthly payments for things I already bought.
- Payoff withheld until the end: The "time-cost formula" showing how interest and fixed payments convert items into mandatory work hours, stripping away your freedom to quit or pivot.

**3. Why the Rich Never Spend Their Own Money**
- Angle borrowed from: *Alux.com* ("How The Rich Live On Loans") and *Corporate Finance Institute* (how financial systems treat debt).
- Hook (first 10 s, Dave speaking, natural sentences): If you use your own cash to buy things, your bank account shrinks and you get taxed on what you earned. But the wealthiest people borrow against what they own to get cash tax-free, playing by a completely different set of rules.
- Why a viewer thinks "that's MY problem": I was always told that taking on debt is dangerous and bad, so why does it seem to make wealthy people richer?
- Payoff withheld until the end: The distinction between consumer debt that drains your paycheck and asset-backed leverage that builds non-taxable wealth without touching your savings.

**4. The Invisible Tax Shrinking Your Paycheck**
- Angle borrowed from: *Five Minute Finance* / *Jacob Clifford* ("How Banks Create Money") and *paddy hirsch* ("Fiat Money").
- Hook (first 10 s, Dave speaking, natural sentences): You already know about the income tax printed on your pay stub, but there's a secret third tax taking money out of your wallet every single day. It happens quietly behind closed doors every time new currency is created out of thin air.
- Why a viewer thinks "that's MY problem": My paycheck hasn't changed, but my weekly grocery bill keeps going up for the exact same food.
- Payoff withheld until the end: How bank money creation dilutes the real value of wage labor, acting as a stealth tax that transfers purchasing power directly from workers to asset owners.

**5. Why Earning More Never Makes You Rich**
- Angle borrowed from: *Business Motiversity* ("I Got Rich When I Understood This") and *The Ramsey Show Highlights* (mechanics of being broke).
- Hook (first 10 s, Dave speaking, natural sentences): Most people assume that getting a raise or a better job is the moment their money problems finally disappear. But unless you catch the hidden trap built into modern life, earning double just means your stress gets twice as expensive.
- Why a viewer thinks "that's MY problem": I finally got a raise at work, but somehow I still have the exact same amount left over at the end of the month.
- Payoff withheld until the end: The system of "lifestyle friction"—how subscription models, upgrade culture, and expanding fixed costs are designed to absorb every extra dollar you earn before you can invest it.
