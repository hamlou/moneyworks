- Angle borrowed from: *Moconomy* and *Five Minute Finance* (unmasking the opaque mechanics of how money is created and degraded by design).
- Hook (first 10 s, Dave speaking, natural sentences): "Every dollar sitting quietly in your checking account is losing a race you didn't even agree to run. You think you're making the safe, mature move by holding cash, but the entire financial machine is engineered to punish you for saving it."
- Why a viewer thinks "that's MY problem": They did the "responsible" thing by stockpiling cash in a bank, yet notice their balance buys less groceries and gas every single month.
- Payoff withheld until the end: Modern currency isn't a vault for wealth—it's a debt token designed to erode at 2% or more per year so citizens are forced to spend or risk their capital to keep the economy from freezing.

- Hook (first 10 s, Dave speaking, natural sentences): "Notice how tapping your phone to buy an overpriced iced coffee doesn't feel like spending anything at all? - Payoff withheld until the end: Removing physical friction from transactions tricks the brain into skipping its survival-based budget check, automatically increasing average consumer spending by up to 30% per swipe.

- Hook (first 10 s, Dave speaking, natural sentences): "Ever wonder why credit card companies and auto lenders rush to hand you loans the second you land your first decent job?
