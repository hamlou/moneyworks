### Taxes — PASSED
- FLORIDA Amendment 3: ~62% yes (60% needed). Homestead exemption on non-school property taxes → $150,000 in 2027, $250,000 in 2028, inflation-indexed after; non-homestead assessment cap 10%→5%. Two other FL amendments failed.
  - Spectrum News 13 2026-11-04: https://mynews13.com/fl/orlando/news/2026/11/04/amendment--raises-florida-homestead-exemption-starting-in-0
  - CBS12: https://cbs12.com/news/local/florida-amendment-3-what-a-yes-or-no-vote-means-for-homeowners-and-local-services

- COLORADO Amendment 87: flat 4.4% income tax → 6 brackets 3.7%–8.4%; raises taxes >$500k earners, modest cuts most others. ~51%, opponents conceded. Competing Prop 136 (cap at 4.4%) failed.
  - Axios Denver 2026-11-05: https://www.axios.com/local/denver/2026/11/05/colorado-voters-approve-amendment-87-graduated-income-tax

- IOWA: 2/3 supermajority for income tax increases — passed (Tax Foundation).

  - Yahoo: https://www.yahoo.com/news/politics/articles/2026-midterms-just-broke-massive-140208470.html
  - The Hill: https://thehill.com/homenews/campaign/6152887-2026-midterm-could-be-most-expensive-ever-adimpact/
- OpenSecrets: total ~$12B projected, "most expensive election in U.S. history." Spectrum 2026-11-02: https://spectrumlocalnews.com/us/snplus/politics/2026/11/02/2026-midterm-election-most-expensive-in-history--12-billion
  - [CONFLICT] 2022 coverage cited $16.7B+ total for 2022 midterms. "$12B most expensive ever" likely different scope. Safer on-screen claim: AdImpact ad record $11.6B.
- Texas Senate: $573M (as of Oct 30) = most expensive non-presidential race on record. Ohio Senate special: $514M. First cycle with 2 non-presidential races > $500M. Michigan Senate $347M (Oct 27). (Yahoo/AdImpact)
- Texas: Talarico raised $145M from individuals vs Paxton $22M; Musk $150M+ backing Paxton (Spectrum/OpenSecrets).
- Elon Musk: $445M to Republican PACs; George Soros ~$100M to Dem-aligned PACs. GOP-aligned groups outspent Dem-aligned by ~$400M (Spectrum/OpenSecrets).
- House: ~$1.66B general-election ads (Oct 30). 3 House races > $50M: NC-1, NY-17, FL-25 (Yahoo).
- Cost per vote (OUR MATH: late-Oct spending ÷ two-candidate votes; floor estimates):
  - Texas: $573M ÷ 9,694,873 ≈ $59 per vote; ≈ $105 per Talarico vote.
  - Michigan: $347M ÷ 4,695,437 ≈ $74 per vote; ≈ $138 per El-Sayed vote.
